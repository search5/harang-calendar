harang-calendar documentation
================================

An `Obsidian <https://obsidian.md>`_ plugin that shows your
`CalDAV <https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4791>`_ calendar events directly
in your notes and in dedicated calendar views.

Reference a date or a specific event with a dedicated inline syntax; it is
rendered as a card widget (a day's events) or a compact chip (one event)
right in the note. A sidebar agenda list and a full-tab month grid give you
two more ways to browse events without leaving Obsidian. The plugin is
**read-only** — it never creates, edits, or deletes anything on your CalDAV
server.
